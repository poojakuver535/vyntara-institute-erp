Admissions 2026-27

Applications Open until December 15, 2026

Admission Process

1. Fill the online application form below.
2. Pay the application fee (₹1500) via net-banking/UPI.
3. Appear for entrance exam (VYNTECH-ET) OR submit JEE/CET score.
4. Shortlisted candidates will be called for counseling.
5. Confirm admission by paying first-term fees.

Eligibility

  • B.Tech: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Math (min 60%)
  • BBA/BCA: 10+2 any stream (min 50%)
  • MBA: Graduation (min 50%) + CAT/MAT/CMAT score
  • M.Tech: B.Tech/B.E. (min 60%) + GATE preferred
