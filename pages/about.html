About Vyntara Institute

Established 2001 • NAAC A+ • NIRF Ranked

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized institution of higher learning, nurturing innovation, excellence, and ethical leadership in science, technology, and management.

Our Mission

To impart quality education through state-of-the-art infrastructure, dedicated faculty, industry partnerships, and research opportunities that empower students to become responsible global citizens.

History

Founded in 2001 by Dr. Rajesh Vyntara, our institute began with just 60 students and 3 programs. Today, we proudly host over 5,000 students across 40+ programs in engineering, management, sciences, and humanities. Our 85-acre campus in Bengaluru is home to one of India's most dynamic academic communities.

Accreditations

NAAC A+, NBA accredited programs, UGC recognized, NIRF ranked top 100.

Research Output

500+ research papers, 50+ patents, 25+ funded projects annually.

Industry Ties

Partnerships with Google, IBM, Infosys, TCS, Wipro and leading MNCs.

Alumni Network

50,000+ alumni across 60+ countries, holding leadership roles worldwide.

Leadership

Chancellor: Dr. Rajesh Vyntara, Padma Shri Awardee
